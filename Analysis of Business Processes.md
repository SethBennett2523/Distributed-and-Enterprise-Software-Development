## Analysis of major business processes and organisational responsibilities 
### Business Overview 
The Bristol Regional Food Network aims to connect local producers with individual customers, community organisations, restaurants and cafes through a shared digital marketplace. The system should reduce the difficulty of buying from several local suppliers while helping producers manage product information, orders and payments. 

### Major business processes and their supporting organisational responsibilities

**Product and Inventory Management**
Producers will maintain product descriptions, prices, stock, seasonal availability and relevant food information, including allergens and best before dates. The system will provide the tools for maintaining, updating and presenting this information. This process is essential to ensure accurate product information to support purchasing decisions, order processing and quality investigations.

**Customer Browsing and Orders**
Customers will be able to browse clearly defined categories (veg, dairy, baked goods, seasonal specialities) and search for specific products to submit orders. The system will present all product information and support orders from multiple vendors, while keeping supplier delivery arrangements and collection procedures clear to vendors and customers. The designated 48-hour preparation time will additionally be made clear to consumer’s pre-purchase. This process connects available products to customer demand and creates the order information needed for traceability. 

**Payment Processing**
During purchases the payments will be securely coordinated and distributed to respective producers while a 5% commission for the marketplace will be taken automatically. Producers will receive transparent settlement records for accounting and tax reporting. All transactions will also comply with current data protection and financial service regulations.  This process ensures all payments are linked to correct orders and the relevant producers to maintain accurate payments and traceability. 

**Incident management**
The system will support the ability for all stakeholders, including producers, administrators and customers, to report and submit concerns. The company will coordinate an investigation and review, and support rapid communication if a food-safety concern arose. 

**Notification and Traceability management**
The system will communicate relevant order, delivery, cancellation, delay and incident updates to users. Producers and marketplace staff provide and update the information to trigger these communications. The system will record notification outcomes and support follow ups where required. This process keeps customers and producers informed of relevant updates while supporting overall producer-customer communication to reduce uncertainty and misunderstandings. 

**Surplus produce management** 
Producers will be able to advertise surplus food and apply last minute discounts through the system. This process supports the networks food-waste reduction objective and depends on the current product availability and individual producers pricing. 

**Environmental and educational content**
The system will provide postcode-based food mile estimates and will allow producers to share seasonal recipes, storage guidance and farm stories. This process supports sustainability awareness, local food education and relationships between producers and customers. 

### Relationship to our selected functionalities (Use Case 3)

**Food Quality Incident Management** sits within the marketplace’s wider incident management process. It covers specifically food-quality concerns including expired food, wrong products and incorrect allergen information. In this pipeline, producer and customer information would be obtained, potential risks thoroughly assessed, products suspended where appropriate, and resolution managed between all parties. This functionality relies heavily on Product and Inventory Management for product and producer information. 

**Customer Traceability and Notification** supports incident management and forms part of the wider Notification Management process. It will use order records to identify which customers purchased affected products, generating appropriate notifications and supports follow ups during case reviews between producers, representatives and customers. It depends on Order and Payment processes to confirm customer claims and contact details. 

The two functionalities work together to ensure that investigating an incident leads to appropriate actions for affected customers. <u>Incident Management<u\> determines whether customer notification is required and provides details of the affected product and the action needed across not only the isolated claim, but the wider marketplace. <u>Notification and Traceability Management<u\> identifies the recipients and returns notification outcomes, and any outstanding follow up actions. An authorised marketplace representative will use this information to decide whether to close the incident or request further work.
